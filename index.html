Common questions

Before you call

Will solar run my borehole pump or cold room?

Usually yes, but it depends on the pump or compressor starting current and how many hours you run it. We measure or estimate the load first, then size panels, batteries and any generator to match, instead of guessing.

Do I need approval to install solar?

Requirements depend on the system size, whether it connects to the grid, and your municipality or supplier. We tell you what applies to your site before you spend money, and prepare the technical information the process asks for.

Can lightning damage my solar, pumps or electronics?

Yes. Exposed rural sites are at real risk in the summer storm season. A lightning risk assessment shows what protection is justified, and proper earthing and surge protection are far cheaper than replacing equipment.

Do you do the installation too?

We design, specify and check the work. When your project needs installation, we work with qualified specialist installers so the system is built to the design you approved.

What should I send to get started?

A recent electricity bill, a photo of your distribution board, your location pin and a short description of what you want to run or protect. That is enough for a first conversation.
