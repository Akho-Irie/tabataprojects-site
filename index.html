Will solar run my borehole pump or cold room?

We answer this for your specific setup on a quick call or WhatsApp chat. Send us what you want to run and we will advise.

Do I need approval to install solar?

This depends on your supply and municipality. Contact us and we will walk you through the requirements for your site.

Can lightning damage my solar, pumps or electronics?

Exposed rural sites are at real risk. We assess your risk and design protection to suit. Send us your location and setup.

Do you do the installation too?

Where a job needs installation, we work with qualified specialist installers while we handle the engineering. Ask us about your project.

What should I send to get started?

A recent electricity bill, a photograph of your distribution board, your location pin and a short description of what you want to run or protect.

About Us

About Tabata Projects

Tabata Projects (Pty) Ltd is a South African engineering company focused on electrical engineering, energy solutions and infrastructure development.

We provide practical engineering services spanning feasibility assessments, design, technical specifications, project support and engineering advisory services.

Our approach is founded on sound engineering principles, professional integrity and a commitment to delivering solutions that respond to the technical and operational needs of our clients.

We aim to build lasting relationships by providing reliable engineering support and contributing to the development of sustainable electrical infrastructure.

Our Mission

To deliver practical, reliable and cost-effective engineering solutions that improve the safety, performance and sustainability of electrical infrastructure. We are committed to technical excellence, professional integrity and building lasting relationships through solutions that respond to our clients' needs.

Our Vision

To become a trusted South African engineering and infrastructure solutions company, recognised for technical excellence, innovation and meaningful contribution to the development of the country's energy and infrastructure sectors.

Our values

Integrity

We act honestly, responsibly and professionally.

Practical Innovation

We seek solutions that are effective, sustainable and fit for purpose.

Client Commitment

We value clear communication, reliability and lasting relationships.

Continuous Improvement
